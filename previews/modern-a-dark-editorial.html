Hero — Why Feature Overview / Ledger UI Mockup

Overview

Relay is a workplace async communication platform built for teams across time zones. Why is a feature inside Relay — a decision ledger where teams log and retrieve the reasoning behind decisions, so context survives handoffs and onboarding.

Problem Space

Information lives everywhere. None of it is findable when it actually matters.

Decisions scatter across many surfaces at once — a message in chat, a comment in a design file, an email reply. Each captures a piece. None of them make context retrievable later.

Where decisions end up

Chat msg 23 of 40
Design file buried comment
Email reply 8 of 14
Notes unsynced

Who pays the cost

The New Joiner

Gets partial answers. Ramp-up is longer than it needs to be.

The Mid-Project Inheritor

Inherits outputs, not reasoning. Wrong assumptions show up as rework.

The Manager

Gets messaged constantly to provide context. The pattern repeats.

"Any solution that requires significant effort at the point of capture will not be used consistently. That is not a discipline problem. It is a design problem."

Ideation — 3 Directions Explored

What we explored before landing on a direction

Direction 01

Single Structured Form

One form, all fields visible upfront. Clear structure — but 9 fields at once feels like homework.

Direction 02

Progressive Disclosure

Start with one field, reveal more. Low barrier — but disorienting. "The form that keeps going."

★ Chosen direction

Direction 03

Two Modes — Quick & Full

Quick Capture: 3 fields, under a minute. Full Entry: complete structured record. Both land in the same searchable ledger.
