Variation C — Tight List + Pulled Insight

Research

What the Research Pointed To

We audited the tools teams already use to manage this problem — before designing anything new. Each category solves part of it. Knowing which part let us design around the gap.

01
Messaging Platforms Teams, Slack

Decisions get buried in threads. The context is there — it just can't be found.

These platforms are built around conversation as the primary artifact, not decisions. A call made inside a 40-message thread lives inside that thread. You can search for keywords, but not for structured reasoning.

02
Documentation Platforms Confluence, Notion

The format is right. The timing is wrong.

Capturing a decision here means switching tools, finding a page, and writing from scratch — after the moment has passed. For most teams under normal workload, it doesn't happen consistently. Motivation collapses the moment the decision is made and everyone moves on.

03
Video Tools Loom

A communication format — not a retrieval system.

Excellent for explaining something complex with tone and context. But you can't query a library of videos for a specific piece of reasoning. A Loom inside a structured decision entry is a different story — but as a standalone artifact, it floats in a folder.

04
Task Management Jira, Asana

Tracks what. Doesn't track why.

Designed to track what is being done and whether it's done. The reasoning behind how the work was scoped — why one direction was chosen over another — is absent by design.

The consistent gap

No existing tool captures a decision as a structured artifact at the moment it happens and makes it retrievable from a central place later.
