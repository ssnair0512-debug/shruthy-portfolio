Why Feature Overview / Ledger UI Mockup

Relay is a workplace async communication platform built for teams across time zones. Why is a feature inside Relay — a decision ledger where teams log, organize, and retrieve the reasoning behind decisions, so context survives handoffs and onboarding.

Information lives everywhere.
None of it is findable when it matters.

Decisions scatter across many surfaces — chat, design files, email, stray notes. Each captures a piece. None of them make context retrievable later.

Where decisions end up

Chat messages msg 23/40
Design file comments buried
Email threads reply 8/14
Stray notes unsynced

Who pays the cost

The New Joiner

Gets partial answers. Ramp-up is longer than it needs to be.

The Mid-Project Inheritor

Inherits outputs, not reasoning. Wrong assumptions show up as rework.

The Manager

Gets messaged constantly for context. The pattern repeats.

"Any solution that requires significant effort at the point of capture will not be used consistently. That is not a discipline problem. It is a design problem."

What we explored before
landing on a direction

We worked backwards from retrieval — what is the minimum someone needs to provide for a decision record to be genuinely useful six months later?

Direction 01

Single Structured Form

All fields visible upfront. Consistent output — 9 fields at once feels like homework.

Direction 02

Progressive Disclosure

Start small, reveal more. Low barrier — but disorienting. "The form that keeps going."

★ Chosen

Direction 03

Two Modes — Quick & Full

3-field Quick Capture or complete Full Entry. Both land in the same searchable ledger.
