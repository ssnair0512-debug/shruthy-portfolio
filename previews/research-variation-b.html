What the Research Pointed To

We audited the tools teams already use to manage this problem — before designing anything new. Each category solves part of it. Knowing which part let us design around the gap.

Tool Where it falls short The gap

Messaging Platforms

Teams, Slack

Built around conversation as the primary artifact. A decision buried inside a 40-message thread is technically present — but practically inaccessible. You can search for keywords; you can't search for structured reasoning.

Conversation,
not decision

Documentation Platforms

Confluence, Notion

Right format, wrong workflow. Switching tools, finding the right page, writing from scratch — all of this after the decision, when context has already passed. Works for disciplined teams. Doesn't work consistently for everyone else.

Friction
at capture

Video Tools

Loom

Excellent for explaining complexity with tone and context. But video is a communication format — not a retrieval system. You can't query a library of Looms for a specific piece of reasoning.

Not
queryable

Task Management

Jira, Asana

Track what is being done and whether it's done. The reasoning behind how the work was scoped — why this direction over another — is absent by design.

What,
never why
Consistent gap No existing tool captures a decision as a structured artifact at the moment it happens and makes it retrievable from a central place later.
