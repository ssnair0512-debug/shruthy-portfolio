Research

What the Research Pointed To

We audited the tools teams already use before designing anything new. Each solves part of the problem — understanding which part let us design around the gap, not into it.

Context buried in threads
01Messaging Platforms

Teams, Slack

"The decision is in there somewhere."

Built around conversation, not decisions. A call made inside a 40-message thread is buried there. You can search keywords — not structured reasoning.

Right format, wrong timing
02Documentation Platforms

Confluence, Notion

"I'll document it properly later."

Switching tools after a decision — when context has already passed — means this works for disciplined teams, but not most teams under real workload.

Can't query a video
03Video Tools

Loom

"Watch the 12-minute recording."

Excellent for explaining complexity with tone and context. But a video is a communication format — not a retrieval system. You can't query a folder of Looms.

What, never why
04Task Management

Jira, Asana

"Ticket closed. Reason: unknown."

Tracks what is being done and whether it's done. The reasoning behind how the work was scoped is absent by design.

The gap No existing tool captures a decision as a structured artifact at the moment it happens and makes it retrievable from a central place later.
