Variation B — Three-Act Story

Problem Space

Information lives everywhere. None of it is findable when it actually matters.

Chat Figma Email Notes

Act 01

It happens across many surfaces at once

A message in chat. A comment in a design file. An email reply. A note from a meeting. Each captures a piece of it.

Act 02

No one surface has the full picture

Each tool holds a fragment. None of them make the context retrievable as a whole — because none were built to capture decisions, only communication.

Act 03

They search. They find fragments — or nothing.

They check the chat, scan email threads, look for a document. They find pieces. They make assumptions to fill the gaps. Sometimes those assumptions lead to work being undone.

The root cause

A missing record of reasoning is invisible. Because it is invisible, it rarely gets identified or fixed. The cost compounds — time spent reconstructing context is time not spent moving forward.

Who pays the cost

Not the person who made the decision — the new joiner, the mid-project inheritor, the manager who is the only one holding the institutional memory and gets pulled in constantly to provide it.
