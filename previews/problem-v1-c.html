Variation C — Who Pays + Search Failure

Problem Space

Information lives everywhere. None of it is findable when it actually matters.

Decisions scatter across many surfaces at once — a message in chat, a comment in a design file, an email reply, a note from a meeting. Each captures a piece. None of them make context retrievable later.

Who pays the cost

The New Joiner

Needs to understand why things are the way they are before they can contribute. Gets partial answers. The ramp-up is longer than it needs to be.

The Mid-Project Inheritor

Picks up work already in motion. Inherits the output of past decisions — not the reasoning behind them. Getting it wrong shows up in rework and friction.

The Manager

Holds more context than anyone. Gets messaged constantly to provide it. Every interruption is time pulled from their own work — and the pattern repeats.
