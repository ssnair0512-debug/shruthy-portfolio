Case Study · Relay · Feature Design

Why —
The Decision
Ledger.

Role UX Designer (Team of 2)
Timeline 8 Weeks
Skills User Research, Competitive Analysis, Interaction Design, Prototyping, AI-Assisted UX
Tools Figma, Adobe Creative Suite

Built for teams who lose context every time someone leaves.

Relay is a workplace async communication platform built for teams operating across different time zones, schedules, and working patterns. Why is a feature inside Relay — a decision ledger where teams log, organize, and retrieve the reasoning behind decisions, so context survives handoffs and onboarding.

Information lives everywhere. None of it is findable when it matters.

Decisions scatter across many surfaces at once — a message in chat, a comment in a design file, an email reply. Each captures a piece. None of them make context retrievable later.

The New Joiner

Needs to understand why things are the way they are before contributing. Gets partial answers. The ramp-up is longer than it needs to be.

The Mid-Project Inheritor

Picks up work already in motion. Inherits the output of past decisions — not the reasoning behind them. Getting it wrong shows up in rework.

The Manager

Holds more context than anyone. Gets messaged constantly to provide it. Every interruption is time pulled from their own work.

Where decisions end up

Chat messages msg 23/40
Design file comments buried
Email threads reply 8/14
Stray notes unsynced
Hero — Why Feature Overview / Ledger UI Mockup
"Any solution that requires significant effort at the point of capture will not be used consistently. That is not a discipline problem. It is a design problem."

Three directions. One chosen.

We worked backwards from retrieval — what is the minimum someone needs to provide for a decision record to be genuinely useful six months later?

01

Single Structured Form

One form, all fields visible upfront. Clear structure — but 9 fields at once feels like homework. Easy to close, rarely returned to.

Ruled out — barrier too high at capture.

02

Progressive Disclosure

Start with a single field. Reveal more as the user continues. Near-zero barrier to starting — but disorienting. "The form that keeps going."

Ruled out — users unsure when done.

03

Two Modes — Quick & Full

Quick Capture: 3 fields, under a minute. Full Entry: complete structured record. Both outputs land in the same searchable ledger.

Chosen — respects where the user actually is.
