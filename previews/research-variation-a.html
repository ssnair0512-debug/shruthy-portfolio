What the Research Pointed To

We audited the tools teams already use to manage this problem — before designing anything new. Each category solves part of it. Knowing which part let us design around the gap.

01 Messaging Platforms
Teams, Slack

Decisions happen inside threads, not alongside them. You can search for keywords — but not for structured reasoning. Context is technically present; practically inaccessible.

Conversation, not decision
02 Documentation Platforms
Confluence, Notion

Right format, wrong timing. Switching tools after a decision — when energy and context have passed — means documentation happens for disciplined teams, not most teams.

Friction at capture
03 Video Tools
Loom

Great for explaining complexity in context. But video is a communication format — not a retrieval system. You can't query a library of Looms for a specific piece of reasoning.

Not queryable
04 Task Management
Jira, Asana

Track what is being done and whether it's done. The reasoning behind how the work was scoped — why one direction was chosen over another — is absent by design.

What, never why
The gap No existing tool captures a decision as a structured artifact at the moment it happens and makes it retrievable from a central place later.
