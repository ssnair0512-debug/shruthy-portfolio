Phase 01
Before Event
Discovery · Coordination · Getting there
Emotional Arc
Actions
Hear about the event
Research on Google / Social Media
Coordinate with friends
Buy tickets online
Drive to event
Key Moments
↓ pain
"How can I plan if I don't have info and planning with friends is HARD!!"
↑ delight
"I can't wait to go now that I have a ticket!"
Opportunities
01Organise planning tools in one place
02Consolidate event info so nothing is scattered
03Make coordination feel less chaotic than a group chat
Phase 02
During Event
Arrival · Participation · Socialising
Emotional Arc
Actions
See fashion shows on the runway
Buy food & drinks
See live performances
Buy merchandise from local brands
Socialise with others
Key Moments
↓ pain
"There's so much traffic and I don't know where to find parking?"
↑ delight
"WOW, I love these clothes!" / "I made a new friend."
Opportunities
01Provide an organised itinerary and map
02Surface local food and brand alternatives
03Enable in-event social discovery
Phase 03
After Event
Departure · Reflection · Sharing
Emotional Arc
Actions
Leave event and drive home
Look at pictures
Talk about event with friends
Post on social media
Wear the things they bought
Key Moments
↓ pain
"There's traffic and crowds when leaving."
↑ delight
"That was so much fun!" / "I'm so glad I bought this shirt."
Opportunities
01Provide exit route options
02Enable post-event sharing and reflection
03Give users a social record of their experience
